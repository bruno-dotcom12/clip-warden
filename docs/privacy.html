Privacy Policy

Clip Warden · Last updated 14 September 2026

The short version

Clip Warden runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Clip Warden server and no Clip Warden account. We do not collect, receive, store, or have any technical means of accessing your videos, your transcripts, your social media data, or your access tokens. Everything stays on your machine.

Who is responsible

Clip Warden is an independent open-source project maintained by Bruno Arantes, an individual developer in Brazil. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What the software handles, and where it stays

When you use Clip Warden, the following data is processed locally, on your own device, and is never transmitted to the maintainer:

  • Video and audio files you point it at, and the clips it renders from them.
  • Transcripts generated from your audio. Transcription runs offline on your machine; the audio is not sent to any transcription service.
  • Campaign rules read from the public campaign pages you give it.
  • Access tokens for any social account you choose to connect. These are stored in a file on your own disk, readable only by your user account.

What leaves your machine, and only when you ask

Clip Warden makes outbound network requests in three situations, all of them initiated by you:

  • Downloading source footage from the archive a campaign brief authorises.
  • Reading a campaign page whose link you supplied.
  • Publishing, if and only if you connected a social account and asked for a clip to be sent. In that case the video file and the caption you approved are sent directly from your machine to that platform's official API, over an encrypted connection. They are not routed through any server of ours.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no usage tracking of any kind.

Platform data we request, and why

If you connect a social account, Clip Warden requests the narrowest permission that makes the feature work:

  • TikTok — video.upload, which allows sending a video to your own account's drafts so that you can review and post it yourself. Clip Warden does not request permission to publish directly, and does not request access to your profile data, your videos, your followers, or your analytics.
  • Instagram — the permissions needed to publish a Reel to your own professional account and to confirm which account is connected. Clip Warden does not read your feed, your comments, your direct messages, or your insights.

Data obtained from these platforms is used solely to carry out the action you requested. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train any model.

How to revoke access

You can disconnect Clip Warden from a platform at any time, directly in that platform's own settings, without needing the software:

  • TikTok: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions → remove Clip Warden.
  • Instagram / Meta: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove Clip Warden.

Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored token.

How to delete your data

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no deletion request to send to us and no database for us to erase. To remove everything Clip Warden has kept:

  • Delete the working directory where it wrote your clips, transcripts and contact sheets.
  • Delete the credentials file where any access token was stored. Its location is documented in the installation instructions.
  • Revoke the app's access in the platform's settings, as described above.

If you believe the maintainer nonetheless holds data about you, write to the contact address below and it will be deleted.

Children

Clip Warden is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required by the platforms it connects to.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date, and the change is recorded in the project's public source history.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to wardenclip@gmail.com.
